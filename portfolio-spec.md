## Website Specification: AI Portfolio

### 1. Overview
This document defines the requirements and design direction for a personal portfolio website targeting internship recruiters. The site will showcase hands-on AI project work, technical and creative skills, and a clear, credible professional identity as an AI-powered product builder.

### 2. Audience & Purpose
- **Primary audience:** Internship recruiters seeking candidates who can build with AI.
- **Problem to solve:** Recruiters need to quickly identify candidates with credible, hands-on AI project experience and the ability to explain their process.
- **First impression:** “I build AI-powered product experiences.”

### 3. Professional Direction & Identity
- **Role:** Early-career AI builder (AI Forward Engineer)
- **Identity:** Creative, technical, process-driven, and transparent about real vs. concept work
- **Vibe:** Interactive and bold, showing creativity alongside technical skill

### 4. Visual Style & Interaction
- **Color palette:** #1A1F2B, #3D4451, #F6F1F5, #B28B9F
- **Site feel:** Interactive story (scrollytelling), inspired by [Nintendo Switch BLFGA](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/switch/blfga/index.html)
- **Motion:** Smooth, non-snappy parallax and fade/move effects; scrolling should feel fluid in both directions
- **Mobile:** Must be fully responsive and work smoothly on mobile devices

### 5. Site Structure & Navigation
- **Multi-page site** (must work on mobile)
- **Main navigation:** Home, Projects, About, Skills, Contact

#### Home
  - Hero section with role statement and value proposition
  - Featured projects (teasers linking to full Projects page)
  - Brief “About” preview
  - Smooth interactive scroll effects

#### Projects
  - All projects listed on a single page
  - Each project includes: screenshots, process notes, and explicit labels for “Concept,” “In Progress,” or “Completed”
  - Interactive elements for exploring project details
  - Some featured projects also appear on the homepage

#### About
  - Focus on both your story and your process
  - Blend narrative with visual/interactive elements

#### Skills
  - Interactive display (e.g., grouped by workflow, expandable/collapsible, or animated)
  - Not just a static list

#### Contact
  - Simple, accessible contact form or clear contact info

### 6. Content & Proof
- **Proof types:** Screenshots and other visual/process evidence
- **Transparency:** Clearly label what is real, conceptual, or in-progress
- **No blog or updates section**

### 7. Features & Accessibility
- Parallax scrolling and scrollytelling (smooth, not snappy)
- Interactive elements in Skills and Projects
- Mobile responsive design

### 9. Inspiration
- [Nintendo Switch BLFGA](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/switch/blfga/index.html) (for smooth, interactive storytelling feel)

### 8. Statement of Professional Direction
- The website will include a clear statement on the homepage (and/or About page) that explains the kind of work you want to do. Example statement:

  > "I want to design and build AI-powered product experiences that combine technical depth with creative, interactive elements. My goal is to prototype, test, and ship features that make advanced technology accessible and engaging for real users."

- This statement should be prominent and set the tone for the rest of the site, helping recruiters immediately understand your professional direction and aspirations.
